# MVP scope

## Required for first meaningful pilot

1. Authenticated tenant workspace; member/role/site administration.
2. Phone-first intake with measurement fields and private evidence upload.
3. Triage and controlled state transitions.
4. Evidence-linked findings and 5-Why investigation.
5. Action owner/due date/status/completion proof.
6. Human effectiveness review; failed/inconclusive review blocks closure.
7. Close/reopen with append-only audit trail.
8. Tenant-scoped closed-case search.
9. PDF export from approved values.
10. Optional, feature-flagged AI suggestions that can fail safely.
11. Security controls, migrations, backup/restore procedure and automated tests.

## Defer until validated demand

- ERP/MES integrations and bi-directional sync.
- Offline writing and conflict-aware synchronization.
- Autonomous cause confirmation, machine control, product release or compliance certification.
- Vector/embedding search, custom workflow builders, complex configurable approval chains.
- Native mobile applications; responsive installable PWA is first.
- Multi-site analytics beyond a simple tenant-scoped dashboard.

## Pilot constraints

Focus on one niche and one defect family, run beside the current process, and record bad AI suggestions. The deck's 3–5 pilots for 8–12 weeks is a target in its staged plan, not a commitment or evidence. Do not replace existing quality/safety controls during a pilot.
